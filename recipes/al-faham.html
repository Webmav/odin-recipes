Al Faham

Description

Al Faham is an incredibly flavorful, tender Arabian-style
grilled chicken. It gets its signature tang and moisture
from a double marinade of yogurt and an aromatic, spiced
green paste.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Lemon juice
  • Black pepper powder
  • salt
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Green chillies
  • Coriander leaves
  • Coriander seeds
  • Cumin seeds
  • Fennel seeds
  • Cloves
  • Green cardamon
  • Cinnamon

Steps

  1. Marintate the chicken
  2. Make the spice paste
  3. Second marintaion
  4. Cook
  5. Serve
