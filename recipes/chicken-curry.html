Chicken Curry

Description

This classic, homestyle chicken curry features tender
chicken pieces simmered in a deeply aromatic, spiced
onion-and-tomato gravy. It is straightforward to prepare
and pairs perfectly with steamed basmati rice, rotis, or warm
naan.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Turmeric powder
  • Red chilli powder
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Whole spices
  • Onions
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Green chillies
  • Tomatoes
  • Coriander powder
  • Cumin powder
  • Garam masala powder
  • Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • Hot water
  • Fresh coriander

Steps of preparation

  1. Marinate the chicken
  2. Bloom the whole spices
  3. Brown the onions
  4. Saute the Aromatics and tomatoes
  5. Cook the Ground spices
  6. Sear the chicken
  7. Slimmer and reduce
  8. Final touch
